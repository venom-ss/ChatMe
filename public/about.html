About ChatMe

Random chat, designed to remember less.

ChatMe is a private random chat for adults who want to meet someone new without creating an account or building a permanent profile. It connects two compatible people for a temporary 1-to-1 conversation.

How random matching works

You choose your own gender and who you would like to meet. ChatMe looks only at people who are online and waiting at that moment, checks whether both preferences are compatible, and randomly pairs a compatible match. There are no public profiles, follower lists, likes, or permanent usernames.

Privacy-first by design

ChatMe does not use an application database for accounts or chat history. Matchmaking information exists only while it is needed in server memory. Once a match is made, the temporary matching choices and public encryption keys are cleared from the matchmaking state.

Temporary conversations

Messages are relayed in real time instead of being saved as a searchable conversation archive. Chat payloads are encrypted in the browser with an ephemeral key created for each match. Accepted files up to 6 MB are also encrypted in the browser and relayed in small chunks.

What ChatMe is not

ChatMe is not a social network, dating profile database, or identity-verification service. It cannot guarantee who is on the other side of a random conversation. Access is limited to adults 18 and older by self-confirmation.

The goal is simple: make meeting a random person easy while collecting and retaining as little application data as practical.
