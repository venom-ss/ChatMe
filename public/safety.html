Safety

Random conversation still needs good judgment.

ChatMe is for adults 18 and older. Random matching means you are talking to someone you do not know, and ChatMe does not verify the identity, age, background, or intentions of a chat partner.

Protect personal information

Avoid sharing your home address, workplace details, phone number, private email, passwords, financial information, government identifiers, travel plans, or anything else that could expose you outside the conversation.

Be careful with files and links

Only accept files you genuinely want to receive. Even when transport is encrypted, a file sent by another person can still contain unsafe or unwanted content. Do not open suspicious downloads or follow links you do not trust.

Leave whenever you want

You never owe a random partner a continued conversation. If a chat becomes uncomfortable, manipulative, threatening, sexually inappropriate, or simply unwanted, use Next or End chat and stop engaging.

Do not rely on identity claims

A stranger can misrepresent who they are. Treat claims about age, location, occupation, relationships, money, emergencies, or identity as unverified unless you independently know otherwise.

Keep money out of random chat

Do not send money, cryptocurrency, gift cards, account credentials, verification codes, or payment information to someone you met through random chat. Urgency and emotional pressure are common warning signs of scams.

18+ access

ChatMe requires users to self-confirm that they are at least 18 years old before entering matchmaking. This is a self-declaration rather than an identity or age-verification system, so users should still exercise caution.

The safest default is to keep the conversation inside ChatMe, reveal as little identifying information as possible, and leave immediately if something feels wrong.
